Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

What this demo stores

Lucky Jackpot is a demonstration game. Account details, demo token balances, theme preferences, Jackpot entry counts, and other gameplay settings are stored in your browser using local storage.

Chat messages

Messages sent through the community chat may be stored by the app server so they can be displayed to other players. Do not share passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in chat.

How information is used

The information is used only to provide the demo features, such as signing you in on this browser, showing your token balance, and displaying shared chat messages. This demo does not process real-money payments.

Data choices

You can remove locally stored account and gameplay data by clearing this site’s browser storage. Server-backed chat messages may remain in the app’s message history.

Questions

For questions about this demonstration, use the Contact page.

This sample policy is provided for a demonstration project and is not legal advice.
